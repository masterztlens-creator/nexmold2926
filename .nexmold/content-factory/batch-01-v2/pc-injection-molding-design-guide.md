## Failure Modes and Root Causes

Material-driven failure can arise when resin behavior is incompatible with the selected geometry or process window.

Tooling-driven failure can arise from inadequate parting, ejection, cooling, cavity balance, or access to critical features.

Process-driven failure can arise when filling, packing, cooling, or cycle conditions do not adequately support the part design.

## Troubleshooting and Corrective Action

Troubleshooting Polycarbonate (PC) Injection Molding Guide should follow a mechanism-first diagnostic sequence.

1. Confirm the observed defect or dimensional deviation.
2. Separate geometry-related causes from tooling and process causes.
3. Verify the material specification and processing assumptions.
4. Inspect the relevant mold architecture and cooling / ejection conditions.
5. Change the smallest number of variables necessary to isolate the dominant mechanism.
6. Validate the corrective action against dimensional, cosmetic and functional requirements.
