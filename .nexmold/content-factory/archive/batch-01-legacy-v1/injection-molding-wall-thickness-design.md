## DFM Decision Path

Review geometry and mold pull direction first; then evaluate wall transitions, draft, ribs/bosses, parting line, gating, venting and ejection. Resolve design-for-manufacturing conflicts before steel is cut.

## Production Validation

Use supplier material data and drawing requirements as inputs. Confirm the production-intent mold, resin, machine and inspection method; validate critical dimensions and cosmetic requirements with documented samples and capability evidence.
